Categorical vs. Numerical Variables
Note: Sometimes, a categorical variable may be encoded numerically. For example:•1 = Monday, 2 = Tuesday, …, 7 = Sunday•1 = January, 2 = February, …, 12 = DecemberHowever, since 7 > 1 but Sunday comes before Monday (or 12 > 1 but December comes before January), we often treat these variables as purely categorical and ignore the numerical implications.
Numerical variables•Value is a number and quantity meaningful for comparisons.•Examples:CostTemperatureNumber of  customersAge
Categorical variables•Value is a label that does not carry quantitative meaning.•Examples:GenderArea/Zip codeMarital statusCollege graduate
