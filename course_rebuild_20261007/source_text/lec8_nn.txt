Conclusion
Data Analytics for Business -- Deep Learning160
•Convolutional neural networks and word embeddings are two deep learning architectures designed specifically for image and text data. They typically outperform using “generic” approaches like feed forward neural networks. •CNN’s are based on the convolution operator, which generates a heatmap of  where a small patch of  pixels (the filter) occurs in the image. •Word embeddings capture the meaning of  words and sentences by using high-dimensional vectors, where distances between vectors encode similarity in meaning.
